# Unmanned Aircraft System Requirements (UASR) - English Translation

## Section 101.7: Standard Operating Conditions
(a) Maximum Operating Altitude:
    (1) No person shall operate an unmanned aircraft above 100 m (328 ft) above ground level (AGL).
(b) Horizontal Boundaries:
    (2) Flight horizontal distance from remote pilot shall not exceed 300 m.
    (5) No person shall operate an unmanned aircraft within 5 km of the movement area of any aerodrome.

## Section 101.23: Aerodrome Proximity
No unmanned aircraft shall enter aerodrome movement zones without prior Air Traffic Control or CAAN authorization.
